Top Book Recomendations

Home Page Banned Books Top Recomendations

There are a lot of different genres of books; Romance, Mystery, Fantasy, Young adult, and many more. My personal favorite genres are Poetry, Thrillers, Young Adult and Science Fiction. Here are my top 5 stand alone recomendations spanning across multiple genres and writing styles.

    Top 5 stand alone books

  • The Sun and Her Flowers by Rupi Kaur
  • Four Days of You and Me by Miranda Kenneally
  • The Perks of Being a Wallflower by Stephen Chbosky
  • Save Our Sins by Phynley Meyers
  • Hunger Games by Suzanne Collins

Many people enjoy reading a series of books rather than a stand alone. Here are my top 5 recomendations for a series.

    Top 5 Series

  • Shatter Me by Tahereh Mafi
  • We Were Liars by E. Lockhart
  • A Thousand Boy Kisses by Tillie Cole
  • Pillow Thoughts by Courtney Peppernell
  • Hold Me by Anna Savas
